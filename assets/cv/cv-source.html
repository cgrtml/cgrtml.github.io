Research Interests

I work on what a robot can know about its own state, and what follows for safety. The same question runs through my published work on language-model reasoning in robots: whether the explanation a system gives for its own decision can be checked by anyone else. Most of my measurements end up being about evidence rather than performance, that is, whether a component that scores well actually changes the system's behaviour, and whether the quantity it estimates is observable at all. I test every claim against the simplest alternative before I report it.
